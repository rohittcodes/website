## The Problem

Creating documentary-style videos traditionally requires significant team resources and multi-day timelines. The challenge was compressing the entire production workflow into something a single person could run in minutes, without producing generic-quality AI output.

## What I Built

3DLabs is an end-to-end system that transforms a topic into a finished, production-ready documentary video in roughly 8 minutes. The pipeline runs through ten sequential stages: script generation, scene planning, image creation, animation, voice narration, sound design, and multi-format export, coordinating over a dozen AI models through a unified orchestration layer.

## Technical Highlights

**Visual Coherence**
Managed prompts and seeds across multiple generation calls to maintain consistent imagery throughout the video. The hard part wasn't calling AI models, it was making the pipeline hold together.

**Model Orchestration**
Built a unified routing layer across Gemini, Replicate (Flux, Seedance, LTX, Minimax), and ElevenLabs, abstracting each provider's quirks behind a single interface.

**Cost Management**
Implemented a pre-flight credit reservation system to prevent runaway inference expenses across long, multi-stage generation runs.

**Multi-Tenancy**
Production-ready authentication and workspace isolation via Clerk, so multiple users can run pipelines concurrently without cross-contamination.

## Stack

Next.js · TypeScript · Google Gemini · Replicate · ElevenLabs · FFmpeg

## Result

- Finished, production-ready videos in ~8 minutes from a single topic input
- Eliminated days of manual production work per video
- Coordinated a dozen+ AI models through one orchestration layer without manual glue work
