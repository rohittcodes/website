## The Problem

Independent workers and small teams spend a disproportionate amount of time chasing invoices, switching between billing tools, and manually reconciling payments across clients. Cash flow visibility is reactive, not predictive.

## What I Built

Ledger is a centralized revenue management platform that automates the financial lifecycle end to end. It handles invoicing, tracks payment status autonomously, forecasts revenue, and sends reminders without manual intervention.

## Core Features

**Multi-Workspace Billing**
Architects a clean separation between client workspaces, each with its own invoice history, payment terms, and compliance state, while surfacing a unified view across all of them.

**Predictive Revenue Forecasting**
Analyzes historical payment patterns per client to forecast expected cash flow. Surfaces anomalies (clients trending toward overdue) before they become a problem.

**Autonomous Reminder Workflows**
Integrated automated reminder sequences triggered by payment status: initial due-date nudge, escalating follow-ups, final notice. No manual tracking required.

**Real-Time Compliance Tracking**
Tracks tax compliance state and invoice validity rules per region, flagging issues before invoices are sent.

**Payments Integration**
Integrated Razorpay for global payment acceptance, supporting UPI, cards, net banking, and international methods with webhook-based status reconciliation.

## Stack

Next.js · TypeScript · PostgreSQL · Prisma · Razorpay · tRPC · Vercel

## Impact

- Reduced overdue invoices through automated follow-up logic
- Gave freelancers a single source of truth for all revenue across clients
- Eliminated context-switching between spreadsheets, email, and payment dashboards
